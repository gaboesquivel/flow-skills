# 0007. Official Flow product identity in PRODUCT.md

Status: accepted (2026-10-27). Amended by [0049](0049-agent-does-the-work.md) (principle 3 becomes human-directed, agent-executed).

## Context

Earlier product copy centered flow-skills as a skill collection. October 24–27 discussions (Flow Starter, Flow Factory, simplified catalog) clarified that Flow is a product-minded methodology for developing software with AI agents—not only portable coding skills. Several earlier statements (lightweight methodology, preserve intent, documented way of working, intent-to-verified-value, improve through use) remain stronger than skill-first framing.

## Decision

- **`PRODUCT.md` owns official Flow identity** — tagline, mission, definition, experience, seven principles, success measure, strategic chain, ecosystem boundaries, and dogfooding rule.
- **flow-skills section** — catalog, install, scope, and catalog-alignment guidance stay in the same file under “This repository: flow-skills” so adopters still find distribution facts in one place.
- **Reserved naming** — “Agentic product factory” language applies to Flow Factory, not Flow or flow-skills.
- **Human-led, agent-assisted** — explicit distinction: agents take more execution; humans retain product direction and consequential decisions.
- **Before adding skills** — evaluate against the strategic chain and the catalog-alignment table; prefer simplification over growth.

## Consequences

- [docs/principles.md](../principles.md) and [docs/methodology.md](../methodology.md) remain elaboration and optional practice; they do not supersede the seven principles in PRODUCT.md.
- Future specs and skills should cite PRODUCT.md when scope or identity is in question.
