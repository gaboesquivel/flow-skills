## Ideas and initiatives

The [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md#idea) owns the meanings of idea and initiative. This guide owns where their information lives and how it connects.

These documents form relationships, not mandatory stages. An initiative may start directly from a known problem. One idea may lead to several initiatives or none; research may inform multiple ideas and initiatives. Small changes may need only a task and an update to current context.

When an idea is taken forward, link it to the initiative or current document that owns the resulting work or direction. Keep its origin and disposition in the idea note; maintain scope, execution, and findings in the initiative. Do not maintain competing descriptions. Significant decisions preserve rationale; product, design, and architecture documents describe the current position.
