## Status rules

- **Exploring:** actively considering a possibility.
- **Deferred:** deliberately postponed, with a reason and revisit condition.
- **Adopted:** accepted direction, linked to its authoritative home; implementation may still be open.
- **Dropped:** explicitly rejected, with a reason and any condition for reconsideration.
- **Unresolved:** recovered material whose disposition cannot be established. Do not infer rejection from omission.

No reviewed idea has been marked deferred or dropped without an explicit decision. A note may distinguish adopted direction from unresolved packaging.

Pruning may shorten or relocate an idea, never silently change its status. Keep a dated reason for future status changes. Preserve substantial ideas as files; small alternatives can remain in a related note.
