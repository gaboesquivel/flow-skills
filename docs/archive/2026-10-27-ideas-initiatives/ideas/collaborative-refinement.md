# Collaborative refinement

Status: Adopted capability; packaging unresolved.

Combine questioning, research, suggested directions, and alternatives to shape initiatives linked to assumptions. One compositional entry point and independently callable capabilities remain options. The earlier product/design/architecture focus model and separate explain/explore/refine/prototype commands are historical proposals, not restored decisions.
