# Flow Skills

Project-specific meanings and essential distinctions.

## Language

**Flow**:
The product engineering process being developed in this project.

**Flow Skills**:
The collection of agent skills being developed to support Flow, using Flow itself to guide development.

**Product Decision Record (PDR)**:
A record of a significant product or experience decision and its rationale. In Flow, this includes significant design decisions; shared design rules belong in DESIGN.md.
_Avoid_: PRD, which describes product requirements rather than a decision record.
