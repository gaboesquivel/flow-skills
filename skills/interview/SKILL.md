# Interview

Clarify what decision the interviews should inform, who can speak to it, and what evidence would change the next step. Read existing product context and prior research before proposing questions.

Design a small, purposeful interview guide: opening context, core questions, and follow-ups tied to the decision. Prefer open questions and concrete past behavior over hypotheticals. Note recruitment constraints, sample limitations, and what the conversations cannot establish (for example willingness to pay from interest alone).

When the user supplies transcripts or notes, synthesize themes, contradictions, and implications. Separate direct quotes and observed behavior from your interpretation. Flag assumptions that still need a different kind of test.

Return the guide, synthesis, limitations, and recommended next activity. Interviews do not authorize implementation.

Optional [jtbd](../jtbd/SKILL.md) or [competitors](../competitors/SKILL.md) skills may deepen related questions when available; they are not prerequisites.
