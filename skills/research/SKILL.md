# Research

Define the question and what decision the answer will inform from the request and existing context. Investigate primary sources: official documentation, source code, specifications, first-party data, or direct observations. Follow material claims to the source that owns them.

Use available browsing, repository, and connected tools. If independent workers are available and delegation is authorized, delegate bounded reading; otherwise perform the research directly. Neither a background agent nor a particular interface is required.

Distinguish observations, inference, proposals, and unresolved claims. Record source dates or revisions when freshness matters. Explain inaccessible sources and conflicting evidence rather than filling gaps with guesses.

Preserve durable findings in the repository's existing research location and index them when that convention exists. Keep a narrow lookup in the response if it does not warrant a file. Summarize the answer, evidence, limitations, and implications for the decision.
