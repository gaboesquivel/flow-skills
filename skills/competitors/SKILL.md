# Competitors

Clarify the decision competitors should inform (positioning, scope, pricing, distribution, or build vs buy). Read existing product context first.

Identify alternatives customers actually consider: direct products, adjacent tools, manual workflows, and doing nothing. For each, note the job it serves, strengths, weaknesses, and why someone chooses or leaves it. Prefer primary sources and the user's knowledge; label web summaries as secondary and time-bound.

Summarize differentiation honestly: where the proposed direction wins, where it is weaker, and what would falsify the positioning. Do not treat feature parity lists as strategy.

Return the landscape, implications, open questions, and recommended next activity. Analysis does not authorize implementation.

Optional [interview](../interview/SKILL.md) or [jtbd](../jtbd/SKILL.md) skills may deepen customer evidence when invoked separately.
